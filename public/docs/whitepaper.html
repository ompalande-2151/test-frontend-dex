Rapidex v3 Core Whitepaper

A Concentrated Liquidity Automated Market Maker Protocol designed for the MST Network ecosystem.

Abstract Rapidex v3 is a noncustodial automated market maker implemented for the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) and MST blockchain networks. In comparison to traditional constant product AMMs, Rapidex v3 provides dramatically increased capital efficiency and fine-tuned concentrated control to liquidity providers, improves custom pool deployment configurations, and establishes a flexible fee and protocol governance structure.
